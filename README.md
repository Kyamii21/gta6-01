**Neon Harbor** — an original open-world crime game for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty. You are Raka, a courier in Cahaya Bay, a neon port city. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the harbor police, and finish Mira's three jobs to own the streets.

- A 5×5 city grid with a harbor, pier and container yard, day/night cycle, neon signs and palm-lined streets
- Drive and steal cars; traffic cars and pedestrians fill the roads and sidewalks
- Wanted level (1–5 stars) with police cruisers that chase and ram, and foot officers who arrest you
- Three story missions with briefings, objectives, timers and cash rewards, plus street cash to find
- Combat on foot: punch and pistol with ammo and reload; busted/wasted consequences that cost cash
- Full HUD with health, cash, wanted stars, live minimap, mission tracker and speedometer
- Synthesized WebAudio sound: engine hum, two-tone sirens, gunshots, pickups and mission jingles — no audio files
- Start screen, pause, save/load via localStorage, keyboard + mouse controls

An original game: its city, characters, vehicles, story, art and sounds are all its own. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD, look with the mouse (click to lock), E to enter or steal a car, F to punch, G to shoot, R to reload, Shift to sprint, Space to brake, P to pause, M to mute.
